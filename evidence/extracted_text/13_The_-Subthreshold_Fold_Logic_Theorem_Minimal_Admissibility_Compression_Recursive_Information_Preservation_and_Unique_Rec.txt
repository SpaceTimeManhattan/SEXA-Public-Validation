Abstract
The mathematical reduction of higher-dimensional systems has historically relied upon projection, embedding,
quotient construction, compactification, tensor decomposition, and manifold collapse. Although these techniques
successfully reduce dimensional complexity, they generally accomplish this reduction by discarding structural
information or by requiring auxiliary reconstruction procedures external to the reduction itself. Consequently,
dimensional reduction has traditionally represented a compromise between operational simplicity and complete
structural preservation.

This paper introduces the Γ-Subthreshold Fold Logic, a theorem-grade mathematical transformation establishing a
fundamentally different class of recursive dimensional compression. Rather than operating upon coordinates, tensors,

or geometric embeddings, the Γ-Subthreshold Fold Logic acts upon recursively admissible logical configurations
within a pre-observational admissibility domain. Dimensional realization is therefore not assumed as a primitive
property but instead emerges only after recursive admissibility has been completely satisfied by an ordered chain of
six irreducible Γ-admissibility operators.

The principal theorem establishes that the complete Γ-admissibility chain performs a unique information-preserving
recursive fold from six irreducible logical operators into a five-dimensional operational manifold while preserving every
recursive invariant necessary for exact reconstruction of the complete 2880-dimensional recursive hierarchy. This
establishes a rigorous mathematical interpretation of the Six-Into-Five Principle, demonstrating that the frequently
misunderstood statement that one may "mathematically force six into five and recover 2880" is not an arithmetic
identity but rather the concise description of a formally defined recursive admissibility transformation.

A second principal result proves that the ordered Γ-chain is minimal. Every proper five-operator subchain fails to
preserve one or more essential recursive invariants governing admissibility, recursive closure, computational
correspondence, logical completeness, or exact reconstructability. Consequently, the complete six-operator Γ
architecture constitutes the unique minimal admissibility system capable of supporting recursive
information-preserving dimensional compression within the proposed framework.

Unlike conventional dimensional reduction methodologies, the Γ-Subthreshold Fold Logic derives lower-dimensional
operational structure directly from admissibility itself rather than from geometric projection. Admissibility therefore
becomes the primary mathematical invariant, while geometry emerges only as a consequence of recursive logical
survival. The resulting operator defines a new mathematical class distinct from projection, embedding,
compactification, quotient construction, tensor decomposition, and manifold collapse.

Existence, uniqueness, minimality, recursive closure preservation, computational correspondence, reconstruction
invariance, and explicit failure conditions are established through constructive theorem-grade proofs. Collectively
these results introduce admissibility-preserving recursive fold operators as a previously unidentified class of
mathematical transformation governing information-preserving dimensional organization across recursively generated
manifold hierarchies.
